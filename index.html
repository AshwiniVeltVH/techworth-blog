Because Specifications Aren't Everything

A phone can have an impressive processor, a huge camera number and a massive battery. That doesn't automatically make it the right phone for you.

TechWorth focuses on the questions that matter before you spend your money:

  • What will I actually use this phone for?
  • Is the extra money buying something I need?
  • How long can I realistically keep it?
  • Is there a cheaper option that already does enough?
  • What should I check before buying during a sale?
