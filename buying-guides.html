SMARTER BUYING

Before You Spend Your Money

The most expensive phone isn't automatically the right phone for you.

Before buying, think about:

  • Budget: What is the maximum amount you're genuinely comfortable spending?
  • Usage: Do you mainly use social media, cameras, gaming, work apps or entertainment?
  • Longevity: How many years do you expect to keep the phone?
  • Software: Do you prefer Android or iOS?
  • Storage: Will the base storage be enough for you?
  • Charging: Is fast charging important to your routine?
Your budget should be a ceiling, not a target.

If a cheaper phone already does everything you need, there is no rule saying you have to spend the full budget.
