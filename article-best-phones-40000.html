Best Phones Under ₹40,000 in India in 2026

What can you realistically expect from a phone around ₹40,000? Here is how to compare performance, cameras, display, battery and software support before buying.

October 2026   •   7 min read
Smartphones Around ₹40,000 A practical guide to choosing a phone without spending unnecessarily.

The ₹40,000 price range includes phones focused on performance, display quality, cameras and long-term use.

A budget of ₹40,000 puts you in an interesting part of the smartphone market.

You are no longer limited to basic phones, but you also don't necessarily need to spend ₹70,000–₹90,000 to get a good everyday experience.

At this price, the important question is not simply “Which phone has the biggest specifications?”

Instead, you should ask which combination of performance, camera quality, battery life, software and build quality fits your usage.

What Should You Expect Around ₹40,000?

Phones in this range can offer features that were once reserved for much more expensive devices.

  • High-refresh-rate AMOLED displays
  • Powerful processors
  • Large batteries
  • Fast charging
  • Good primary cameras
  • 5G connectivity
  • Better software support than many budget phones

But not every phone will be equally strong in every area. A phone might prioritise gaming while another focuses more heavily on cameras or software.

OnePlus Nord 5

The OnePlus Nord 5 is an interesting option for buyers who care heavily about performance and battery life.

OnePlus lists the Nord 5 from around ₹33,999 in India.

It features a high-refresh-rate AMOLED display, a large 6800mAh battery, 80W charging and a Snapdragon 8-series processor.

It also comes with a 50MP front camera and 50MP rear camera setup.

What Stands Out

  • Strong performance
  • 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Large 6800mAh battery
  • 80W charging
  • Long software-support commitment

Things to Consider

  • Camera priorities may differ from iPhone buyers
  • Design and software preferences are personal
  • Sale prices can change

Samsung Galaxy A27 5G

Samsung's Galaxy A27 5G is another option for people who want to stay comfortably below the ₹40,000 mark.

Samsung launched the phone in India from around ₹28,999.

It comes with a 6.7-inch 120Hz Super AMOLED display, a 5000mAh battery and Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 processor.

Samsung has also emphasised long-term software support, making it relevant for people who prefer keeping a phone for several years.

What Stands Out

  • 120Hz Super AMOLED display
  • 5000mAh battery
  • Long software-support commitment
  • Samsung software ecosystem

Things to Consider

  • Performance is not the same as higher-end gaming phones
  • Charging speed may matter to some buyers
  • Camera expectations should match the price range

Camera: Don't Buy Based Only on Megapixels

One of the easiest ways to get confused while buying a smartphone is comparing megapixel numbers alone.

A camera's real-world performance depends on much more:

  • Sensor quality
  • Image processing
  • Stabilisation
  • Lens quality
  • Low-light processing
  • Video processing

If photography is important to you, look at actual sample photos and reviews rather than only reading the camera specification.

Gaming and Performance

If gaming is your main priority, processor performance, cooling and sustained performance matter more than simply looking at the phone's RAM number.

The Nord 5 is particularly interesting for buyers who want strong performance without moving into premium flagship pricing.

For casual gaming, however, spending the full ₹40,000 may not be necessary.

Display Quality

A good display can make a phone feel much more expensive than its price suggests.

AMOLED panels with high refresh rates are now common in this segment.

If you spend several hours watching YouTube, scrolling Instagram or reading, display quality is something you will notice every day.

Battery Life

Battery capacity is another area where mid-range Android phones can be impressive.

The Nord 5's 6800mAh battery is substantially larger than what you will find in many compact premium phones.

But battery size should still be considered alongside processor efficiency, display settings and charging speed.

Software Support Matters More Than People Think

If you plan to keep a phone for four or five years, software support should be part of your buying decision.

A cheaper phone with poor long-term support may not be as attractive if you plan to keep it for many years.

Always check the manufacturer's current support policy for the exact model before buying.

Do You Actually Need to Spend ₹40,000?

This is probably the most important question in this entire article.

A ₹40,000 budget does not mean you have to spend ₹40,000.

If a ₹29,000 phone already gives you the performance, display, camera and battery you need, keeping the remaining money is also a perfectly reasonable outcome.

Don't upgrade just because the budget allows it.

Spend more when the additional features actually matter to your daily use.

Before Buying, Ask Yourself These Questions

  1. Is performance more important than camera quality?
  2. Do I play demanding games?
  3. Do I need a very good camera for photos or video?
  4. How many years do I plan to keep the phone?
  5. Is fast charging important to me?
  6. Do I prefer Android or iOS?
  7. Can I spend less and still get everything I need?

Final Thoughts

The ₹40,000 segment is interesting because you don't necessarily have to spend the full amount to get a capable smartphone.

The OnePlus Nord 5 focuses heavily on performance, display and battery life, while the Samsung Galaxy A27 5G offers a different balance around the ₹30,000 mark.

The right choice depends on which features you actually use.

Instead of asking: “Which phone is the best?”

Ask: “Which phone gives me what I need without making me pay for things I won't use?”
